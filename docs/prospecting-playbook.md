# Wireless Bot Prospecting Playbook

## Mission

Wireless Bot helps you find businesses, founders, agencies, and project managers who may need stronger digital execution.

The goal is not to spam people. The goal is to identify real gaps, offer useful help, and position yourself as a capable full-stack developer who can improve business outcomes.

---

## Best target categories

Start with businesses where a better website can directly improve revenue, trust, bookings, enquiries, or customer education.

Good categories:

- Hotels and apartments
- Clinics and pharmacies
- Real estate companies
- Law firms
- Restaurants and lounges
- Schools and training centers
- Radio, TV, and media companies
- NGOs and foundations
- Churches and ministries
- Event planners
- Logistics companies
- Car dealers
- Travel agencies
- SaaS founders
- Startup founders
- Agencies that need extra developer support
- Project managers who need creative technical hands

---

## Strong buying signals

A business may need your service if:

- Their website footer still shows an old year
- Their website is not mobile-friendly
- Their website has no proper title or meta description
- Their homepage is slow
- Their site has no clear call-to-action
- Their site has no WhatsApp/contact button
- Their Google Business profile has no website
- Their social page is active but website is poor
- Their website looks abandoned
- They post often but do not convert traffic to enquiries
- They are hiring developers but may need freelance support
- Their SaaS landing page is weak
- Their project looks active but lacks polish

---

## Outreach principle

Do not sound desperate.
Do not insult their current website.
Do not say: your website is bad.

Say something like:

> I noticed a few areas where your current online presence can be improved to help visitors understand your services faster and contact you more easily.

---

## Suggested offer packages

### Starter Website Upgrade

For businesses with old websites.

- Homepage redesign
- Mobile responsiveness
- SEO metadata
- WhatsApp CTA
- Contact form
- Google Analytics/Search Console setup

### Business Growth Website

For businesses without strong online presence.

- Full business website
- Service pages
- Blog/news section
- Lead capture form
- SEO structure
- Social media integration

### Enterprise Platform Support

For SaaS founders, agencies, and project managers.

- Laravel/PHP development support
- Frontend implementation
- Dashboard UI
- Bug fixing
- Feature development
- Technical documentation
- Deployment support

---

## Follow-up rhythm

Day 1: First message
Day 3: Helpful follow-up
Day 7: Soft reminder
Day 14: Final value message

Keep it human. Keep it specific. Keep it useful.
